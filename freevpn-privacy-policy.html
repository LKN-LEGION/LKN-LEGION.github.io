Pupper VPN — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app com.FGStudio.FreeVPN. Last updated: 16 September 2026.

The short version. We do not keep logs of what you do through the VPN. We do not record the sites you visit, your DNS queries, or the contents of your traffic. There are no accounts, so there is nothing that ties a tunnel to your identity. The app is paid for by ads, and the ad SDK — not the VPN — is the only part that sees an advertising identifier.

1. Who we are

Pupper VPN is developed and operated by FG Studio. Contact: genryk75@gmail.com. We operate the VPN servers ourselves; the tunnel is not resold or routed through a third-party VPN provider.

2. What the VPN service stores

To run the tunnel at all, the server has to hold a small amount of technical state. It is kept in memory and on the server only while your session is alive, and it is deleted when the session ends or expires.

We do not store: visited domains or URLs, DNS query logs, packet contents, a history of your real IP addresses, or any per-user traffic archive.

3. Content filtering

The server resolves DNS for everyone on the tunnel and blocks adult (18+) and malware domains from a public blocklist. Search engines and YouTube are not modified: there is no forced SafeSearch and no restricted mode. Filtering happens in real time; queries are answered and discarded, not logged. If you need a completely unfiltered connection, this app is not the right tool.

4. Ads

The app shows ads through CAS.AI (Clever Ads Solutions) and its mediation partners. The ad SDK may process your advertising identifier (AD_ID), coarse device and app information, and ad interaction events. This is standard for ad-supported apps and is handled by the SDK, not by us — we never receive your browsing activity from it.

Ads never play while the tunnel is up: the app pauses the VPN for the video and reconnects afterwards. Your VPN traffic is therefore never visible to an ad network.

You can review or withdraw your advertising consent at any time from the in-app menu ("Ad settings"), and you can reset or delete the advertising identifier in your Android settings.

5. Usage statistics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to count app opens and whether connection attempts succeed, together with technical device and app data that Firebase processes (such as the app version, device model and an app-instance identifier). Visited sites, server IP addresses and VPN keys are never included, and the advertising identifier is not used for analytics.

Where the GDPR applies, analytics storage follows the choice you make in the consent dialog shown before the first ad. Everywhere, you can turn statistics off at any time in the app menu ("Usage statistics"); turning them off also clears the analytics data stored on the device.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • VPN connection — granted by you in an Android system dialog; required to create the tunnel.
  • Notifications — to show the ongoing session notification with the countdown, speed and a disconnect action.
  • Internet / network state — to reach the VPN server and detect connection changes.

The app does not request access to contacts, files, location, camera, microphone, or SMS.

7. Children

Pupper VPN is not directed at children. The app is rated for users aged 18 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families programme.

8. Sharing and legal requests

We do not sell personal data and we do not share VPN activity with anyone, because we do not have it. If we receive a legal request for user activity, we can only answer with what exists: aggregate server metrics and, for a live session, the technical state listed in section 2.

9. Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time from the app or from the notification — session state is deleted on disconnect.
  • Turn usage statistics off (or back on) in the menu ("Usage statistics").
  • Change or withdraw ad consent in the menu ("Ad settings").
  • Uninstall the app — nothing remains on our side once your session expires.
  • Ask us anything about your data at genryk75@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we store, we will update the date at the top of this page and describe the change in the app's release notes.
