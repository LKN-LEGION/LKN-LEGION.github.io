CO-OP · ACTION · PHYSICS

Why to Fish:
Online

Fishing is only the beginning. Team up online, travel between six islands by boat, catch unusual fish, and face a giant boss on each island. Ragdoll physics, collisions, and explosive accidents can turn even a quiet trip into a memorable mess.

Play solo or with friends, earn rewards, upgrade your equipment, and experiment with 22 unlockable weapons. The Google Play listing describes online play for up to eight players.

Take precise control of a vehicle on obstacle courses high above the ground. Narrow roads, steep ramps, sharp corners, moving hazards, and big jumps make every checkpoint feel earned.
