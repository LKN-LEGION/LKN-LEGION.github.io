The Game does not ask you to register, log in, or give us your name, e-mail address, phone number or payment details. There are no in-app purchases. Online play works through an anonymous identifier tied to your device.

2a. Voice chat and the microphone

Online rooms have voice chat. The Game asks for the microphone permission only when you first use voice chat and listens to the microphone only while voice chat is active. Your voice is sent live to the other players in the same room and played on their devices. It is not recorded, stored, transcribed or sent to any analytics or advertising service. You can turn voice chat off, or deny / revoke the microphone permission in your Android settings at any time.

4. Advertising and your choices

The Game is free and shows ads. Ads may be personalised based on your advertising ID. When required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states) the Game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads);
  • change your consent choice in the consent window shown by the Game;
  • stop all collection by uninstalling the Game.

5. Data security and retention

Data sent by the Game and its third-party services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). We do not keep data on our own servers. Third-party services keep data for the periods described in their own policies.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.
