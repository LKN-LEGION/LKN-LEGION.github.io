Privacy Policy - Voxplode

Developer: FGStudio · Contact: 777even999@gmail.com
Effective date: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how FGStudio ("we", "us") handles information when you use the mobile game Voxplode (package com.fgstudio.voxplode, the "Game"). By using the Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or location.
  • You do not need an account. Your progress is stored on your device.
  • The Game shows advertisements and uses analytics and crash reporting. These services use anonymous device identifiers.
  • The online mode uses an anonymous device login with Epic Online Services.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

  • to provide the Game, save progress and run the online mode;

  • to keep the Game secure and prevent fraud and abuse;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

We process data to perform our agreement with you (providing the Game), on our legitimate interests (analytics, security, non-personalised ads) and, for personalised advertising and certain identifiers, on your consent. Where consent is required, the Game asks for it before personalised ads are shown (through a consent window from our advertising partners). You can change your choice at any time in the Game's settings where available, or by resetting the advertising ID on your device.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Apps will then not be able to use it for personalised ads.
  • Offline play: the online identifier is created only when you open the online mode. You can play the sandbox and the contracts offline (ads and analytics need a connection to work).
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the Game deletes everything stored on your device. To request deletion of data held by us or our partners, e-mail us (section 11).
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. We do not sell personal information for money. To exercise any right, contact us; you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and contains cartoon destruction of buildings and vehicles. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention

Progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game. Analytics and advertising data are kept by our partners for the periods described in their policies (see each partner's policy). Online room data exists only while the room is open.

9. International transfers and security

Our partners may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States, with safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Data in transit is encrypted by these services. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose partners that use industry-standard protection.

10. Changes to this policy
