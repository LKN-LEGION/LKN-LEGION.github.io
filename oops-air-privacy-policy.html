The Game does not ask you to register, log in, or give us your name, e-mail address, phone number or payment details. There are no in-app purchases. Online play works through an anonymous identifier tied to your device.

2a. Voice chat and the microphone

Online rooms have push-to-talk voice chat. The Game asks for the microphone permission only the first time you press TALK, and listens to the microphone only while you hold that button. Your voice is compressed and sent live, through the room's host, to the other players in the same room, and played on their devices. It is not recorded, stored, transcribed or sent to any analytics or advertising service. You can turn voice chat off in Settings, mute any other player from the pause menu, or deny / revoke the microphone permission in your Android settings at any time.
